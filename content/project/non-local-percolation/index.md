I study novel percolation processes with non-local rules, in which the fate of an edge depends on global properties of the network rather than on local ones.

I investigated **shortest-path percolation**, a process where edges are removed in a spatially and temporally correlated fashion, and revealed markedly different critical behaviour on Erdős–Rényi and scale-free networks.
